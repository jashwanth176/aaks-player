Woodford XR. Generated by tools/make_controller.py, vertex coloured, no texture.

Shipped as models/controller.room, drawn at each hand's grip when "Show the
controllers" is on.

Rebuild from the repository root (the glb is an intermediate, not kept):

  python3 tools/make_controller.py controller.glb
  python3 tools/bake_room.py controller.glb \
      app/src/main/assets/models/controller.room

  controller.room   58,300 bytes, 890 vertices, 1,592 triangles, one part
                    painted from its vertex colours, no atlas

Right hand, OpenXR grip space, metres: origin mid-handle, +x right, +y up,
-z forward. The renderer mirrors it in x for the left hand.
